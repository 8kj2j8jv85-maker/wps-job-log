Comments on your report

Found something in the property that isn't in the report, or disagree with something in it? Tell us here, one point at a time, with a photo of each. We check every point against our photos and let you know the outcome.

How it works

  1. Have your report open. You'll need the job number (top of the first page, e.g. WPS261001) and the item numbers (the "No" column on the left of each room).
  2. Fill in your details.
  3. Add one point for each issue, one item per point: the room, the item number, what's wrong, and at least one clear photo.
  4. Not in the report at all? Tick "This item is not in the report" and say where it is.
  5. Press Add another point for the next issue, then Send comments.

Good to know

Send your comments within 7 days of receiving the report. After 7 days the report is deemed agreed by all parties.
