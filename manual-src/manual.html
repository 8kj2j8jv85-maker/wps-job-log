Opening the tools

All four live at one web address. Bookmark it in Chrome on the PC and on the phone:

https://8kj2j8jv85-maker.github.io/wps-job-log/

When Google asks you to sign in, always choose info@wellingtonparker.com. That's the account that holds the Job Log Sheet, the calendar and the inbox the tools read.

Where your jobs are kept

Your jobs are saved in two places at once:

  • In the browser on each device, so the pages open fast and work without a signal.
  • In the Google Sheet "WPS Job Log" on info@. This is the master copy that keeps the PC and the phone in step.

On the PC every change saves to Google by itself within a few seconds. The sidebar then shows Saved to Google ✓ 08:45 (with the time). On the phone, tap Sync now each time you open it (see section 11).

Don't edit the Google Sheet by hand. Make every change in the Job Log. The Sheet is written by the Job Log and will be overwritten.

Help hints

There are two kinds of help built into the pages:

  • Hover hints. On the PC, rest the mouse on any button, tile or pill for a second and a short explanation pops up. These are always on.
  • Help hints switch. In the sidebar under TOOLS, Help hints: on ✓ shows a yellow "How this works" box at the top of each page and a short line of text under the main buttons. That works on the phone too, where there's no hovering. Click it again to switch the hints off once you know your way round. The switch applies to the Dashboard, Job Log, Photo Tool and Dictation together.

The Manual link just under the switch opens this manual.

2. Your day, step by step

This is the normal rhythm. Each step is explained in more detail later in the manual.

Morning

1
Open the Dashboard. Look at the tiles across the top. Red numbers are the urgent ones.
2
Click Check for new bookings. Claude checks the inbox at 7:51, 12:51 and 15:51 (Monday to Saturday) and lines up anything that looks like a booking, a change or a cancellation. Go through each card and add, change or dismiss it (section 7).
3
Click the Times to agree tile. These are booked jobs with no time yet. Contact the tenant and add the time to the job.
4
In the Today box, click Today's route to open Google Maps with today's jobs as stops, in time order.

After each visit

5
Click the job's Booked pill once, so it moves to Processing. That starts the report clock: check-outs are due 48 hours later, everything else 72 hours.
6
On the phone, use Dictation as you walk round (section 10). Back at the PC, run the day's photos through the Photo Tool (section 9).

When a report is finished and sent

7
Move the pill to Completed when the report is written, then to Sent the day it goes out. For an inventory and check-in, "Sent" means sent for signature in Acrobat (with the comments form link in the email). The 7-day comment window starts the moment you click Sent.
8
Keep an eye on the Comment windows and Amendments tiles for check-ins (section 8). When Acrobat's signed copy comes back, move the pill to Signed.

Money

9
The To invoice tile lists reports sent but not invoiced. Raise the invoice in Tide, then click the job's invoice pill once (it changes to Invoiced) and type the invoice number into the job. When the money arrives, click the pill again for Paid.

Once a week

10
Click Export backup in the sidebar to save a copy of every job to the PC. Glance at the On time tile to see how quickly reports are going out.

4. Job stages (the coloured pills)

Every job has a stage, shown as a coloured pill. Clicking the pill moves the job one step forward. It never goes backwards from a click.

StageMeansMove it here when…
BookedIn the diary, not visited yet.Automatic for new jobs.
ProcessingVisited, report being written. The report due time is counted from when you click this.You've done the visit.
CompletedReport written, not sent yet.The report is finished.
SentReport has gone out. The sent date is recorded. For check-ins, the 7-day comment window starts. The job also joins "To invoice".The day you send it (or send it for signature).
SignedCheck-in report signed.Acrobat's signed copy comes back.
Deemed agreedCheck-in window closed with no signature and no open comments.You don't: it's set by itself (section 8).
ArchivedFinished and filed. Drops off the Dashboard lists.The job is fully done.

Clicking goes Booked → Processing → Completed → Sent → Signed → Archived. A Deemed agreed job goes to Archived.

Check-outs, mid-terms, schedules and clean checks aren't signed. Once they're sent and invoiced, click the pill twice (Signed, then Archived), or open the job and set Status to Archived.
Clicked one step too far? Open the job (click its address), change the Status box back to the right stage and click Save changes. Moving a job back before Sent clears its sent date.

Report due and overdue

Once a job is visited (Processing, or Booked with the visit time passed), it gets a due time: 48 hours for check-outs, 72 hours for everything else. You'll see "Report due Wed 16:00" under the pill, amber when it's within 6 hours, and red "Report overdue 16h" when late. Overdue rows in the Job Log are shaded red.

If lots of jobs show as overdue, it's usually because reports were sent but the pills weren't moved along. Move each one to Sent (or further) and the count drops.

Click Job Log in the sidebar. It shows every job, with the ones needing a report first, then the newest.

1Count cards. How many jobs at each stage, plus overdue, to invoice and awaiting payment. Click a card to show only those jobs. Click it again to show all.
2List / Calendar. Switch between the list and a month calendar.
3Search. Type part of an address, postcode, agent, tenant name or email, job number or invoice number.
4Filters. Show one job type, one agent or one month.
5Stage pill. Click to move the job on one step. The line underneath says when the report is due.
6Invoice pill. Click for Not invoiced → Invoiced → Paid (a fourth click goes back to Not invoiced). It shows "To invoice" in coral once the report is sent.
7Pencil. Open and edit the job.
8Bin. Delete the job. It asks first. There's no undo, and it also removes the calendar entry.
9Calendar icon (only when the calendar is connected). Puts the job on the info@ calendar, or updates it. Blue = already on the calendar.

Calendar view

  • 1 Prev / Next change month. 2 Today jumps back to this month.
  • 3 Today has a coral border. Click an empty part of any day to add a job on that date.
  • 4 Click a job to open it. Hover over it on the PC to see the full address.

6. Adding and editing a job

Click + New job (top right of the Dashboard or Job Log), the + on a day, or an empty calendar day. To change a job, click its address or the pencil. The same form opens either way.

1Paste box. Paste the agent's booking email and click Extract job details. It fills in the date, address, postcode and job type it can find. It works best when the address and postcode sit on their own line. Always check what it fills in.
2Job number. Fills itself: WPS + year + month + number, e.g. WPS261015. Numbers are never reused, even after a delete. You can type over it (e.g. for an old Parker number).
3Status. The stage. Change it here to move a job backwards.
4Invoice and invoice number (from Tide).
5Agent / client. Pick from the list. Choose + Add new agent… at the bottom to add one.
6Agent contact. The saved contact for that agent. Click Edit agent contact details to change it. It's saved for every job with that agent.
7Tenant emails. Separate two tenants with a comma. Used for the 7-day reminder on check-ins. Fill it in on every check-in.
8Copy to (agent / landlord). Who gets the "amendments in progress", "signed" and "deemed agreed" emails. Several addresses, separated by commas. Left blank, the agent's saved email is used.
9Save job (or Save changes). If the calendar is connected, the job goes on the info@ calendar too. Cancel closes without saving.

The other boxes: date and time of the visit, property type, bedrooms, extra areas, street address, town, postcode, tenant names and phone (who to contact for access), and notes (access details, key collection and so on).

Re-issuing a check-in report after amendments

When you've corrected a check-in report after tenant comments and sent it again, open the job. Under Status you'll see when its window closes and a button:

Click it the day you re-send. The job goes back to Sent and a new 7-day window starts, covering the changed items only. The reminder wording changes to match.

8. Comment windows, reminders and amendments

This only applies to inventory and check-in reports. Tenants have 7 days from when the report is sent to send comments through the comments form. If they don't comment or sign, the report is deemed agreed.

The Comment windows tile

  • Each line shows when the report was sent and when the window closes. Amber means 2 days or less left.
  • 1 At 2 days left a coral Reminder due: write email button appears. It opens a ready-written reminder in Gmail, addressed to the tenant emails on the job. Check it, put the tenant's first name in, and send. The line then says "Reminder sent" with an undo link if you clicked by mistake.
  • When the automatic reminder emails are switched on (see section 13), it says "Reminder sent automatically" instead, and you don't need to do anything.

When the window closes

  • No comments open: the job moves to Deemed agreed by itself. This only happens after the amendments have been loaded (below), so a job with comments waiting is never deemed agreed by mistake.
  • Comments still open: it shows On hold: amendments. Deal with the comments, re-send, and click Re-issued today on the job (section 6).
  • Not checked yet: "Deemed agreed (pending)" means the amendments haven't been loaded today. Click the Amendments tile and load them.

The Amendments tile

Click the tile, then Load amendments. Google may ask you to sign in. It reads the WPS Amendments Sheet and lists every job with tenant comments still open:

  • To work through them, say "review amendments WPS261005" (with the job number) to Claude in the WPS project chat on the PC. Claude fills in the review page.
  • 1 Open review page is where you decide each point: Make the change, Add to Notes or No change.
  • Amendments Sheet opens the raw list. Refresh reads it again.

9. The Photo Tool

Use it on the PC, after a day of visits. It does four things for each job: splits the day's photos into jobs, names them by room, adds the watermark, and makes one zip per job. Everything happens on the PC. Your original photos are never changed.

Before you start: open the Photo Tool from the sidebar, in the same Chrome you use for the Job Log, so it can see your jobs. On the iPhone, set Settings › Camera › Formats to Most Compatible so it shoots JPG. HEIC photos work but lose their "time taken", which the tool relies on.

Step 1: drop the photos in

Drag the whole day's photos onto the dashed box, or click it and choose them. A whole Send Anywhere batch is fine.

Step 2: check each set

The photos are split into sets wherever there's a gap of 30 minutes or more between shots. Each set is matched to a job by where the photos were taken, or by booking time if the photos have no location.

1How it matched. Green "Matched by location" is reliable. Amber "Matched by booking time — check" or "Location match — check" means look at it. "Not matched — pick the job" means choose one.
2Job Log. The job this set belongs to. Jobs near where the photos were taken are listed first, with the distance. Pick a different one if it's wrong.
3Job number and postcode. Fill in from the job. You can type them by hand. The zip button stays grey until both are there.
4Names preview. What the zip and photos will be called, e.g. WPS261006.E39ZF.zip and WPS261006.E39ZF.12.10.26.Kitchen_01.jpg.
5Process & download zip for this job (step 4).
6Areas check. "All areas set" in green, or how many photos still have no room.
7Photos. ▶ in coral marks the first photo of a room. The grey label on the following photos shows they belong to that room.
8This batch. How many photos and jobs, and that it can see the Job Log.
9Process all jobs makes every zip in one go. Below it, + Add more photos (anything you missed) and Start again (clears the tool, originals untouched).
  • One visit split in two (you stopped for a long time): click Merge with job above on the second set.
  • Two visits joined together (they were close in time): open the first photo of the second visit and click Start a new job from here.
  • You went back to the same property later: pick the same job on both sets. They're put into one zip.

Step 3: mark the rooms

Click the first photo of each room. A panel opens at the bottom:

1Room buttons. Click the room. Every photo after it belongs to that room until the next one you mark. The panel then moves to the next photo, so you can carry on.
2Other area. Type a room that isn't in the list (e.g. "Bedroom 1 (1st LHS)") and click Set or press Enter. It's added to the list for next time.
3Rotate left, right or upside down.
4Remove photo leaves it out of the zip. It isn't deleted from your PC. Click again to put it back.
5Start a new job from here splits the set at this photo.
6Close (or Esc).

Keyboard shortcuts in the panel: ← → previous/next photo, R rotate, Del remove, Esc close. To rotate several at once, click one photo, then Shift-click another to select the run between them (or Ctrl-click to pick photos one by one), and use the buttons in the panel.

The External picture room is special: as well as going in the zip, a clean copy (no watermark, larger) is saved separately as the report cover photo, e.g. WPS261006.E39ZF.cover.jpg.

Step 4: process and download

Click Process & download zip on the set (or Process all jobs). A progress bar shows each photo. When it's done:

  • The zip and the cover photo are in your Downloads folder.
  • The photo link is saved to the job in the Job Log by itself, which clears it from the "Photos not done" tile. Copy link copies it for the report.
  • The tool doesn't upload the zip. Put it on the photos server as you do now, so the link works.

Inside each photo: the date and time it was taken are kept, the location is removed, and a small navy Wellington Property Services tag with the date and time sits in the corner.

Settings

Click Settings at the bottom of the sidebar. Settings are saved on that PC.

  • 1 New job after a gap of (minutes). Default 30. Lower it if two nearby visits get joined.
  • Photo size, JPEG quality, how portrait photos are handled, canvas colour.
  • 2 Watermark position, size and text. Click Preview watermark to see it.
  • 3 Photo link starts with. The web address the link is built from.
  • 4 Area list. The room buttons, one per line, in the order you usually walk a property.
  • 5 Save settings. Reset to defaults puts everything back. Load Job Log backup is only needed if the tool says it can't see your jobs.

10. Dictation

Use it on the phone during a visit. Open it from Dictation under LINKS in the sidebar (on the phone, it's under Menu ☰). Add it to the home screen so it opens like an app. The notes are kept on that phone only, until you copy them out.

Top bar:

  • 6 Home goes back to the Dashboard.
  • 1 ? shows or hides the help lines.
  • 2 Jobs: your saved dictations, and Start a new job.
  • 3 Settings: word fixes, rooms, audio backup.

Steps:

  1. 4 Type the job number, pick the type, add the address.
  2. 5 Tap a room. A red dot means it already has notes.
  3. 1 (right picture) Tap the microphone and talk. Tap again to stop.
  4. 2 The words appear in the box. You can type or correct them.
  5. Tap the next room and carry on.
  6. 3 At the end tap Copy for Claude and paste into the WPS project chat. 4 Share sends the notes to another app.

While talking you can say "full stop", "comma", "question mark", "new line", "new paragraph" or "next item" (starts a new bullet). Clear (next to the room name) empties that room's text.

  • Corrections: when the phone keeps mishearing a word, add a line like skirting bored = skirting board. It's fixed automatically from then on.
  • Rooms: the room buttons, one per line, in walk-round order.
  • Keep audio backup: keeps a recording of each room on the phone, to replay if the text came out wrong. If the live text stops working with this on, switch it off.
  • Export all notes saves a backup file. Tap Save settings when done.
The microphone needs permission the first time. If the phone asks, tap Allow. The live text comes from the phone's own speech recognition, which usually needs a signal.

11. Using it on your phone

The Dashboard and Job Log work on the phone and tablet. The sidebar folds into a bar at the top, and each job shows as a card.

First time on a new phone or tablet

  1. Open the web address in Chrome (or Safari on the iPhone).
  2. Tap Load my jobs from Google and sign in as info@wellingtonparker.com. Your jobs appear.

Every time you open it

  • 1 Tap Sync now. It says Syncing… then Synced ✓ with the time. That brings over anything you changed on the PC.
  • After you change something on the phone, tap Sync now again so the PC gets it.
  • If it says Sync again ⚠, tap it again. The reason shows underneath (often the Google sign-in was closed).
  • 2 Menu ☰ opens the links and tools. Close ✕ folds them away.

The Photo Tool is meant for the PC (big batches of photos and downloads). Dictation is meant for the phone.

13. What runs by itself, what you do

Runs by itself

  • Inbox check at 7:51, 12:51 and 15:51, Monday to Saturday: lines up bookings and flags new amendments.
  • Saving the Job Log to Google (PC, within seconds).
  • Job numbers.
  • Report due times and overdue warnings.
  • The 7-day comment window countdown on check-ins.
  • Deemed agreed, once amendments are loaded and none are open.
  • Calendar entry when you save a job (if connected).
  • Photo link saved to the job by the Photo Tool.
  • Four automatic emails from reports@ (amendments in progress, signed, deemed agreed, 7-day reminder to tenants). These are still in test mode: they only go to reports@, marked [TEST]. Nothing reaches agents or tenants until test mode is switched off.

You do

  • Check the bookings tray and add, change or dismiss.
  • Agree times and add them.
  • Move the stage pills along after each visit and report.
  • Fill in tenant emails and "Copy to" on check-ins.
  • Send reports, and check-ins for signature in Acrobat (with the comments form link).
  • Send the 7-day reminder from the dashboard button (until the automatic emails go live).
  • Load amendments, and ask Claude to "review amendments" for a job.
  • Run the Photo Tool and put the zips on the photos server.
  • Copy dictation to Claude.
  • Invoice in Tide and click the invoice pill.
  • Sync now on the phone. Export backup weekly.

15. Known gaps

Things that aren't finished yet, as of 12 October 2026:

  • Automatic emails are in test mode. Until switched on, send reminders yourself from the Comment windows tile.
  • Cornell (WPS260921) was marked Sent before send dates were recorded, so it has no comment window on the dashboard.
  • Photo links still use wellingtonparker.com. The new photos.wellington-ps.co.uk address isn't working yet.
  • On the phone you have to tap Sync now each time. It doesn't sync by itself.
  • Extract job details only reads a clean address line. When the address is buried in a sentence, type it in.
  • The job form doesn't show the photo link. It's saved and clears the "Photos not done" tile, but you see it in the Photo Tool (Copy link), not on the job.
  • The Photo Tool and Dictation were checked at phone and tablet size in a test browser, not yet on your own devices.

If this manual and the tools ever disagree, the tools are right. Ask Claude to update the manual.
