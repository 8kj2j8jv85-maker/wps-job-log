Opening the tools

The Dashboard, Job Log, Photo Tool and Dictation live at one web address. Bookmark it in Chrome on the PC and on the phone:

https://8kj2j8jv85-maker.github.io/wps-job-log/

When Google asks you to sign in, always choose info@wellingtonparker.com. That's the account that holds the Job Log Sheet, the calendar, the inbox and the Drive folders the tools use.

The web address follows the screen. The Job Log is …/wps-job-log/#log, the calendar #calendar, and an open job #job=WPS261006. So the browser's Back button steps back through what you looked at, refreshing keeps you where you were, and you can bookmark a job.

After Claude changes the tools, press Ctrl+Shift+R on the PC to load the new version. An open tab keeps the old one, and the website can take up to about 10 minutes to hand out the new copy.

Where your jobs are kept

Your jobs are saved in two places at once:

  • In the browser on each device, so the pages open fast and work without a signal.
  • In the Google Sheet "WPS Job Log" on info@. This is the master copy that keeps the PC and the phone in step. It also holds your clients, your saved contacts (the Copy to list) and the bookings you've already dealt with, so another device doesn't offer them again. The report auto-send reads it too.

On the PC, once Google is signed in, every change saves to Google by itself within a few seconds. The sidebar then shows Saved to Google ✓ 08:45 (with the time). If it says Google copy: click Save & get latest, the tab isn't signed in yet: click Save & get latest. On the phone, tap Save & get latest each time you open it (section 14).

Don't edit the Google Sheet by hand. Make every change in the Job Log. Anything typed straight into the Sheet is ignored and will be overwritten. The sidebar link says so: Job Log Sheet (view only, don't edit).

Help hints

  • Hover hints. On the PC, rest the mouse on any button, tile or box for a second and a short explanation pops up. These are always on.
  • Help hints switch. In the sidebar under TOOLS, Help hints: on ✓ shows a yellow "How this works" box at the top of each page and a short line of text under the main buttons. That works on the phone too, where there's no hovering. Click it again to switch the hints off. The switch applies to the Dashboard, Job Log, Photo Tool and Dictation together.

The Manual link just under the switch opens this manual.

2. Your day, step by step

This is the normal rhythm. Each step is explained in more detail later in the manual.

Morning

1
Open the Dashboard and click Check everything now. In one go it saves to the Google Sheet and gets the latest copy, checks for new bookings, picks up reports the auto-send has emailed, and loads the amendments. Google may ask you to sign in: choose info@.
2
If anything was found, a To review tray opens at the top. Claude checks the inbox at 7:51, 12:51 and 15:51 (Monday to Saturday) and lines up new bookings, changes, cancellations and Tide invoices you've sent. Go through each card (section 8). Nothing goes in the log until you click.
3
Look at the tiles. Red numbers are the urgent ones. The list at the bottom opens on the most urgent tile by itself.
4
Click the Times to agree tile. These are booked jobs with no time yet. Contact the tenant and add the time to the job.
5
In the Today box, each job shows the tenant and the notes (keys, access). On the phone, tap a phone number to call it. Today's route opens Google Maps with today's jobs as stops, in time order.

After each visit

6
Move the job to Processing: pick it in the job's stage box, or click Mark visited in the Reports due list. Check-outs are due 48 hours after the visit, everything else 72 hours.
7
Visit didn't happen? Click Rearrange on that job in the Reports due list (section 4).
8
On the phone, use Dictation as you walk round (section 13). Back at the PC, run the day's photos through the Photo Tool (section 12).

When a report is finished

9
Set the stage to Completed when the report is written.
10
Check-outs, mid-terms and schedules of condition: save the PDF into the Drive folder WPS Ready to send (section 9). Within about 15 minutes it is emailed from reports@ to the right people, and the job is marked Sent the next time you click Check everything now. If something is missing, the PDF is held and info@ gets an email saying why.
11
Inventories and check-ins: for now, still send them for signature in Acrobat with the comments form link (the WPS signing page isn't live yet, section 10). Then set the stage to Sent. The 7-day comment window starts from the sent date. When Claude sends a check-in for you, Claude marks it Sent with a link and you click Confirm and save.
12
Keep an eye on the Comment windows and Amendments tiles for check-ins (section 11). When Acrobat's signed copy comes back, set the stage to Signed.

Money

13
The To invoice tile lists reports sent but not invoiced. Raise the invoice in Tide and put the WPS job number in the invoice's message line (or note). Tide emails a copy to info@; the next inbox check turns it into an Invoice sent card in the To review tray. Click Mark invoiced and the job is set to Invoiced with the invoice number and date.
14
When the money arrives, open the Awaiting payment tile and click Paid on that job.

Once a week

15
Click Export backup in the sidebar to save a copy of every job to the PC. Look at the Performance panel to see how the week went (section 3).

6Tiles, in four groups: To do now, Waiting on others, Money, Performance. Click a tile to show its list in box 9. A tile with nothing in it is faded.

Reports completed

Under the date, Reports completed counts your finished reports this month, this year and all time. An inventory and check-in counts once it's Signed, Deemed agreed or Archived; every other report counts once it's Sent (or later). It only counts jobs in the Job Log, so it's WPS work only.

Milestones. When the all-time count passes 50, 100, 250, 500, 1,000, 2,000 or 3,000, a card appears under the panel. Click Thanks to put it away. Each device shows each milestone once.

The Performance panel

Click the On time tile. At the top, a message card: it picks its words from how the month is going (a personal best, up on last month, a quiet spell, or steady). Underneath, four cards:

  • On time this month: the share of reports sent within target, how many, the average hours, and last month's figure once there is one.
  • Reports completed this month: compared with last month up to the same day, with ▲ (up) or ▼ (down).
  • Nothing overdue streak: how many days in a row with no report overdue. It drops to 0 when one is overdue today.
  • Personal bests: your busiest week and your fastest turnaround.

The figures are worked out from the dates the Job Log has kept since 11 October, so older jobs may be missing from them.

Themes

The Theme box in the sidebar changes the Dashboard's colours on that device: Classic (the usual look), Light (white background) or Night (dark, easier on the eyes in the evening). Each device remembers its own choice.

Marking an invoice Paid

Click Paid and the job is marked Paid with today's date. An Undo bar shows for 8 seconds in case you clicked the wrong one (section 5). Open in Job Log shows the same jobs in the full list.

4. Missed visits: Mark visited and Rearrange

Click the Reports due tile. The list has two parts:

  • Visit time passed, still at Booked. The visit time has gone but the stage wasn't changed. These aren't counted as overdue.
    1 Mark visited: you did go. The job moves to Processing. The report countdown still runs from the visit time, not from the click.
    2 Rearrange: the visit didn't happen.
    If the report has already gone out, use the job's stage box instead.
  • Reports to write. Visited jobs, with the due time ("Report due Wed 16:00", amber inside 6 hours, red "Report overdue 22h" when late). Jobs at Processing also have a Rearrange button, in case the visit didn't really happen.

Undo

Every stage or invoice change from a box, Mark visited and Paid shows a bar at the bottom of the screen for 8 seconds, e.g. "WPS261002 marked Paid". Click Undo to put the job back exactly as it was. Missed it? Pick the old stage (or invoice state) in the box again. Moving a job back before Sent clears its sent date.

Report due and overdue

Once a job is visited (Processing, or Booked with the visit time passed), it gets a due time: 48 hours for check-outs, 72 hours for everything else, counted from the visit. You'll see "Report due Wed 16:00" under the stage box, amber when it's within 6 hours, and red "Report overdue 16h" when late. Overdue rows in the Job Log are shaded red.

If lots of jobs show as overdue, it's usually because reports were sent but their stage wasn't changed. Set each one to Sent (or further) and the count drops. Reports emailed by the auto-send are set to Sent for you.

7. Adding and editing a job

Click + New job (top right of the Dashboard or Job Log), the + on a day, or an empty calendar day. To change a job, click its address or the pencil. The same form opens either way.

1Paste box. Paste the agent's booking email and click Extract job details. It fills in the date, address, postcode and job type it can find. It works best when the address and postcode sit on their own line. Always check what it fills in.
2Job number. Fills itself: WPS + year + month + number, e.g. WPS261015. Numbers are never reused, even after a delete. You can type over it (e.g. for an old Parker number).
3Status. The stage, the same as the stage box in the lists.
4Invoice and Invoice number (from Tide). Usually filled by Mark invoiced (section 8).
5Agent / client. Pick from the list. Choose + Add new agent… at the bottom to add one. The box underneath shows that agent's saved contact; Edit agent contact details changes it for every job with that agent.
6Tenants. One row per tenant: name, phone, email. + Add tenant adds a row (up to 6); ✕ removes one. The emails get the signing links on check-ins (section 10), the 7-day reminder and the Rearrange emails. A tenant email that doesn't look right stops the save until it's fixed. Older jobs that had tenants typed as one line are split into rows for you, with a pink "Split from the old text" line: check the rows, then save.
7Copy to (agent / landlord). Tick who gets the reports the auto-send emails (unless a client rule says otherwise, section 9) and the "amendments in progress", "signed" and "deemed agreed" emails. The job's agent's contacts are listed first; Other contacts folds the rest away. + Add contact saves a new name and email and ticks it. Remove contacts lets you delete saved contacts (jobs that already copy to them keep the address). The line underneath says who will get the emails; with nothing ticked they go to the agent's saved email.
8Photo link. Saved by the Photo Tool, or paste a link (e.g. Dropbox) by hand. The auto-send puts it in the report email. Open opens it, Copy copies it for the report email. It must start with http:// or https://. Clear the box and save to remove it. A link the Photo Tool saves while the form is open is kept, unless you changed the box.
9Delete job (existing jobs only). Asks first, removes the calendar event too, can't be undone. To keep a record, set Status to Archived instead.
10Save job (or Save changes). If the calendar is connected, the job goes on the info@ calendar too. Cancel, the browser's Back button or the Esc key close it without saving. If you've changed anything, it asks first: OK throws the changes away, Cancel keeps you editing. Leaving for another page or closing the tab with unsaved changes also asks first.

The other boxes: job type, date and time of the visit, property type, bedrooms, extra areas, street address, town, postcode, and notes (access details, key collection and so on).

Calendar events are titled "<job number> - <type> - <address>". A job with a time gets a timed event, a job without a time gets an all-day event; changing between the two updates the same event. If Google refuses, a message names the job and gives Google's reason.

8. The To review tray: bookings, invoices, sent reports

Three times a day (7:51, 12:51 and 15:51, Monday to Saturday) Claude reads the info@ inbox (mail to every WPS and old Wellington Parker address lands there) and lines up:

  • New bookings, including short requests like "check-in at 402a on the 17th" (the month, address and postcode are completed from the sender's older emails and flagged).
  • Changes to a booking, including tenant details sent after the booking.
  • Cancellations.
  • Tide invoices you've sent (the copy Tide emails to info@).

It also picks up the tenants' names, phones and emails, and takes the person who sent the instruction as the Copy to. If a works order looks copied from an old one (old tenant details), it leaves the tenants out and says so on the card. WhatsApp bookings can't be seen: add those by hand.

9. Sending reports automatically

Check-outs, mid-terms and schedules of condition are emailed for you. Inventories and check-ins go out through the signing page (section 10). You only save the finished PDF into one folder.

How to send a report

1
Check the job in the Job Log: right client, type, address and postcode, and a photo link. On a check-in, check the tenant rows have emails.
2
Save the finished PDF into the Drive folder WPS Ready to send. On the HP it is G:\My Drive\WPS Ready to send (Google Drive for desktop, signed in as info@). The file name must have the job number in it once, e.g. WPS261007.E32BX.12.10.26.MTI.pdf.
3
That's it. Every 15 minutes the script looks in the folder. A file saved in the last 3 minutes waits for the next run, so a half-copied file is never sent.

What happens when it sends

  • The report is emailed from reports@wellington-ps.co.uk with the PDF attached and the photo link in the email. A check-out email carries both the check-out and the check-in photo links.
  • Who gets it: Winkworth - Bow reports go to bowpropman@winkworth.co.uk, starting "Hi Riley,". For other clients it goes to the people ticked in the job's Copy to, or if none, the client's email on the Clients tab, starting "Hello,". Mid-terms always copy in bowpropman@winkworth.co.uk.
  • The PDF moves to WPS Ready to send › Sent.
  • A "Report sent" confirmation goes to reports@ (filed under Reports).
  • A line is added to the Report sends tab of the WPS Amendments Sheet.
  • The next time you click Check everything now, the job is set to Sent with the time it was emailed.

When a report is held

If anything is missing or doesn't match, nothing is sent. The PDF moves to WPS Ready to send › Held and info@ gets an email "Report held, not sent - WPS…" saying why. Fix the problem, then move the PDF from Held back into WPS Ready to send. It's picked up within 15 minutes. The script never guesses a recipient or a link.

10. The check-in signing page

Inventory and check-in reports are signed on WPS's own signing page instead of Acrobat. It went live on 18 October 2026, 01:38. You send a check-in the same way as any other report: save the PDF into WPS Ready to send (section 9).

Before you save the PDF

  • The job is an Inventory & Check-In in the Job Log, with 1 to 6 tenant rows that have emails.
  • The client is right. Clients whose name starts with Winkworth, Move Lettings, McQueens, Chestertons or JAGS are letting agents: they get a copy, they don't sign. Any other client is treated as a private landlord, who signs too and needs an email on the Clients tab. To skip the landlord, put Landlord signs: no in the job notes.
  • The report is made from the ICI_2 template and its last page is the Declaration with the six Name / Signature / Date rows. Not password-protected.

If any of these isn't right, the PDF is held and info@ is told why, as in section 9.

What happens

1
Each tenant (and a private landlord) gets their own email from reports@ with the report attached and a Review and sign the report button. Each link works only for that person, so tenants are never copied in on each other's emails. The email also gives the comments form link and the date the 7-day window closes.
2
The agent gets a separate "sent for signature" email with a copy of the report. reports@ is copied in.
3
On the page, the tenant reads the report, confirms their name, types or draws a signature and ticks the declaration. They get a "signature received" email.
4
Two days before the window closes, anyone who hasn't signed gets one reminder with their own link. (For these jobs this replaces process email D.)
5
When everyone has signed, a signed copy is made within 15 minutes: names, signatures and dates on the declaration page, plus a signing record page at the end. Your original PDF is never changed. It's saved in WPS Ready to send › Signed and emailed to every signer and the agent, with reports@ copied in ("Report signed and completed - WPS…").
6
You set the job to Signed when that email arrives. The Dashboard doesn't do it by itself yet.

Where to see who has signed

The Signings tab in the WPS Amendments Sheet has one row per signer: when the link was sent, first opened, signed, and how (typed or drawn).

  • Need to send a corrected report? Put RESEND in the file name and save it into the folder again. A new signing round starts and the old links stop working.
  • Nobody signs and no comments are open? Deemed agreed works as before (section 11).
  • If the signed copy can't be made, info@ gets "Signed copy NOT finished" with what to do. Tell Claude.
The first real check-in on the signing page hasn't been watched end to end yet. Tell Claude when you save it, and Claude will check info@'s Sent mail (one email per tenant, and the agent's copy) and the Signings tab.

Acrobat stays available (sidebar link) for anything the signing page can't take, for example more than 6 tenants.

11. Comment windows, reminders and amendments

This only applies to inventory and check-in reports. Tenants have 7 days from when the report is sent to send comments through the comments form. If they don't comment or sign, the report is deemed agreed.

The Comment windows tile

Each line shows when the report was sent and when the window closes. Amber means 2 days or less left.

The 7-day reminder. At 9am each day the reminder email to tenants goes out by itself from reports@ for any check-in with 2 days or less left, as long as the job has tenant emails. Check-ins sent through the signing page get the signing page's own reminder instead (section 10). The line then says "Reminder sent automatically" and you don't need to do anything.

If it hasn't gone (for example no tenant emails were on the job), 1 a coral Reminder due: write email button appears. It opens a ready-written reminder in Gmail, addressed to the tenant emails on the job. Pick reports@ as the From address, check it and send. The line then says "Reminder sent" with an undo link if you clicked by mistake.

When the window closes

  • No comments open: the job moves to Deemed agreed by itself. To be safe this only happens when the amendments were loaded in the last 30 minutes, so a job with comments waiting is never deemed agreed by mistake. With Google signed in, the page re-reads the amendments quietly when it refreshes.
  • Comments still open: it shows On hold: amendments. Deal with the comments, re-send, and click Re-issued today on the job (section 7).
  • Not checked yet: "Deemed agreed (pending)" means the amendments haven't been loaded recently. Click Check everything now, or the Amendments tile and Load amendments.

The automatic emails to the agent or landlord (the job's Copy to) are covered in section 16.

The Amendments tile

Click the tile, then Load amendments (Check everything now does this too). Google may ask you to sign in. It reads the WPS Amendments Sheet and lists every job with tenant comments still open:

The comments form

Tenants send comments through the comments form (the link in the report email and on the signing page; Amendments form in the sidebar). The form only accepts a real WPS job number with the property's matching postcode. Photos must be JPEG. The receipt goes only to emails already on the job.

Working through the comments

To work through them, say "review amendments WPS261005" (with the job number) to Claude in the WPS project chat on the PC. Claude fills in the review page.

12. The Photo Tool

Use it on the PC, after a day of visits. It does four things for each job: splits the day's photos into jobs, names them by room, adds the watermark, and makes one zip per job. Everything happens on the PC. Your original photos are never changed.

Your work is kept. You can go to the Dashboard or another page and come back, or close and reopen the tab: the photos, sets and rooms you've marked are kept on that device until you click Start again. The page shows Kept on this device ✓ — safe to switch pages.

Before you start: open the Photo Tool from the sidebar, in the same Chrome you use for the Job Log, so it can see your jobs. On the iPhone, set Settings › Camera › Formats to Most Compatible so it shoots JPG. HEIC photos work but lose their "time taken", which the tool relies on.

Step 1: drop the photos in

Drag the whole day's photos onto the dashed box, or click it and choose them. A whole Send Anywhere batch is fine.

Step 2: check each set

The photos are split into sets wherever there's a gap of 30 minutes or more between shots. Each set is matched to a job by where the photos were taken, or by booking time if the photos have no location.

1How it matched. Green "Matched by location" is reliable. Amber "Matched by booking time — check" or "Location match — check" means look at it. "Not matched — pick the job" means choose one.
2Job Log. The job this set belongs to. Jobs near where the photos were taken are listed first, with the distance. Pick a different one if it's wrong. The list only offers jobs dated from 21 days before to 2 days after the photos.
3Job number and postcode. Fill in from the job. You can type them by hand. The zip button stays grey until both are there.
4Names preview. What the zip and photos will be called, e.g. WPS261006.E39ZF.zip and WPS261006.E39ZF.12.10.26.Kitchen_01.jpg.
5Process & download zip for this job (step 4).
6Areas check. "All areas set" in green, or how many photos still have no room.
7Photos. ▶ in coral marks the first photo of a room. The grey label on the following photos shows they belong to that room.
8This batch. How many photos and jobs, and that it can see the Job Log.
9Process all jobs makes every zip in one go. Below it, + Add more photos (anything you missed) and Start again (clears the tool, originals untouched).
  • One visit split in two (you stopped for a long time): click Merge with job above on the second set.
  • Two visits joined together (they were close in time): open the first photo of the second visit and click Start a new job from here.
  • You went back to the same property later: pick the same job on both sets. They're put into one zip.

Step 3: mark the rooms

Click the first photo of each room. A panel opens at the bottom:

1Find an area. Start typing (e.g. "bed") and the room buttons narrow down to the ones that match. A name that isn't in the list (e.g. "Bedroom 1 (1st LHS)") gets an Add button: click it or press Enter and it's added to the list for next time.
2Room buttons, in walk-round order. Click the room. Every photo after it belongs to that room until the next one you mark. The panel then moves to the next photo, so you can carry on. Remove room start undoes a room you marked by mistake.
3Rotate left, right or upside down.
4Remove photo leaves it out of the zip. It isn't deleted from your PC. Click again to put it back.
5Start a new job from here splits the set at this photo.
6Close (or Esc).

Keyboard shortcuts in the panel: ← → previous/next photo, R rotate, Del remove, Esc close. To rotate several at once, click one photo, then Shift-click another to select the run between them (or Ctrl-click to pick photos one by one), and use the buttons in the panel.

The External picture room is special: as well as going in the zip, a clean copy (no watermark, larger) is saved separately as the report cover photo, e.g. WPS261006.E39ZF.cover.jpg.

Step 4: process and download

Click Process & download zip on the set (or Process all jobs). A progress bar shows each photo. When it's done:

  • The zip and the cover photo are in your Downloads folder.
  • The photo link is saved to the job in the Job Log by itself, which clears it from the "Photos not done" tile. You can see it, open it and copy it in the job form too (section 7). Copy link copies it for the report.
  • The link is only saved to the job when the job was picked from the Job Log list. If you typed the job number by hand, paste the link into the job form yourself.
  • The tool doesn't upload the zip. Put it on the photos server as you do now, so the link works.

Inside each photo: the date and time it was taken are kept, the location is removed, and a small navy Wellington Property Services tag with the date and time sits in the corner.

Settings

Click Settings at the bottom of the sidebar. Settings are saved on that PC.

  • 1 New job after a gap of (minutes). Default 30. Lower it if two nearby visits get joined.
  • Photo size, JPEG quality, how portrait photos are handled, canvas colour.
  • 2 Watermark position, size and text. Click Preview watermark to see it.
  • 3 Photo link starts with. The web address the link is built from.
  • 4 Area list. The room buttons, one per line, in the order you usually walk a property.
  • 5 Save settings. Reset to defaults puts everything back. Load Job Log backup is only needed if the tool says it can't see your jobs.

13. Dictation

Use it on the phone during a visit. Open it from Dictation under LINKS in the sidebar (on the phone, it's under Menu ☰). Add it to the home screen so it opens like an app. The notes are kept on that phone only, until you copy them out.

Top bar:

  • 6 Dashboard · Job Log · Photo Tool links go straight to those pages.
  • 1 ? shows or hides the help lines.
  • 2 Jobs: your saved dictations, and Start a new job.
  • 3 Settings: word fixes, rooms, audio backup.

Steps:

  1. 4 Type the job number. If the job is in the Job Log on that phone, the address and type fill in by themselves. Otherwise pick the type and add the address.
  2. 5 Tap a room. A red dot means it already has notes. The coral ring shows the next room to do.
  3. 1 (right picture) Tap the microphone and talk. Tap again to stop.
  4. 2 The words appear in the box. You can type or correct them.
  5. Tap the next room and carry on.
  6. 3 At the end tap Copy for Claude and paste into the WPS project chat. 4 Share sends the notes to another app.

Rooms from the last visit

When the address (with its flat or house number and postcode) matches a property you've dictated before on that phone, the room buttons are filled in from that visit, in the same order, and a line says which visit it was. A check-out uses the check-in's rooms; a mid-term or schedule of condition uses the latest visit. A new property shows the full room list. More rooms adds the full list after them; + Other adds a room. The rooms only change before you start dictating, so nothing you've said is lost.

While talking you can say "full stop", "comma", "question mark", "new line", "new paragraph" or "next item" (starts a new bullet). Clear (next to the room name) empties that room's text.

  • Corrections: when the phone keeps mishearing a word, add a line like skirting bored = skirting board. It's fixed automatically from then on.
  • Rooms: the room buttons, one per line, in walk-round order.
  • Keep audio backup: keeps a recording of each room on the phone, to replay if the text came out wrong. If the live text stops working with this on, switch it off.
  • Export all notes saves a backup file. Tap Save settings when done.
The microphone needs permission the first time. If the phone asks, tap Allow. The live text comes from the phone's own speech recognition, which usually needs a signal.

16. What runs by itself, what you do

Runs by itself

  • Inbox check at 7:51, 12:51 and 15:51, Monday to Saturday: lines up bookings, changes, cancellations and Tide invoices, flags new amendments, and writes reply drafts in Gmail for you to check (it never sends).
  • Report auto-send every 15 minutes: check-outs, mid-terms and schedules saved into WPS Ready to send are emailed from reports@, or held with an email to info@ saying why (section 9). The job is set to Sent at the next Check everything now.
  • Check-in signing page: signing links to each tenant, the agent's copy, one reminder 2 days before the window closes, and the signed copy to everyone once all have signed (section 10).
  • Saving the Job Log to Google (PC, within seconds, once signed in).
  • Job numbers.
  • Report due times and overdue warnings.
  • The 7-day comment window countdown on check-ins.
  • Deemed agreed, once amendments have been loaded in the last 30 minutes and none are open.
  • Calendar entry when you save a job (if connected), and moving or removing it when you rearrange.
  • Photo link saved to the job by the Photo Tool (when the job was picked from its list).
  • The Dashboard refreshing itself every 3 minutes.
  • Four automatic emails from reports@, live since 12 October:
    A "amendments in progress" when the first comments come in for a job;
    B "report signed and completed" when Acrobat's "Signed and Filed" email arrives (checked every 15 minutes);
    C "report deemed agreed" (9am daily);
    D the 7-day reminder to tenants with 2 days left (9am daily; signing page jobs get their own reminder instead).
    A, B and C go to the job's Copy to, or the agent's saved email if none is ticked. D goes to the tenant emails. B skips anyone who signed in Acrobat. For signing page jobs, the signed copy email is B.
  • The reports completed counter, milestones and the Performance panel.

You do

  • Check everything now, then check the To review tray: add, change, mark invoiced or dismiss.
  • Check and send the reply drafts the inbox check left in Gmail.
  • Add WhatsApp bookings by hand.
  • Agree times and add them.
  • Mark visits done, or Rearrange the ones that didn't happen, and check and send the Rearrange email.
  • Set the stage after each visit, and Completed when a report is written.
  • Check client, type, photo link, tenant rows and Copy to before a report goes into WPS Ready to send.
  • Save finished reports into WPS Ready to send. Fix and move back anything held.
  • Send by hand anything the auto-send doesn't take (other report types, PDFs over 24 MB, more than 6 tenants).
  • Set check-ins to Signed when the signed copy arrives.
  • Send the 7-day reminder from the dashboard button only when the automatic one hasn't gone.
  • Load amendments, and ask Claude to "review amendments" for a job.
  • Run the Photo Tool and put the zips on the photos server.
  • Copy dictation to Claude.
  • Invoice in Tide with the WPS number in the message line; click Paid when paid.
  • Save & get latest on the phone. Export backup weekly.

18. Known gaps

Things that aren't finished or haven't been tried in real use yet, as of 18 October 2026:

  • On the phone you have to tap Save & get latest each time. It doesn't sync by itself.
  • Signed is set by hand. The Dashboard can't see Acrobat or the signing page yet.
  • The first real auto-sent report and the first real check-in on the signing page haven't been checked end to end yet.
  • The auto-send doesn't check the photo link opens; it uses the link on the job or page 1 of the report.
  • WhatsApp bookings can't be seen by the inbox check.
  • Rearrange emails haven't yet been checked in a real Gmail window: the From line (should be hello@), the signature, and the date box showing dd/mm/yyyy. Check them on the next real rearrange.
  • After a rearrange with no date, setting the date later leaves the note saying "New date to agree". Edit the note if it matters.
  • Apply change copies Claude's summary into the job notes (e.g. "needs moving"), which reads out of date once applied.
  • A tab not signed in to Google saves only in the browser; the calendar catches up on the next save after sign-in.
  • The calendar error message hasn't been seen on a real failure yet.
  • "Changed both here and on another device" was seen once on 13 October; which device caused it isn't known.
  • The Photo Tool and Dictation don't read the Google Sheet. The Photo Tool sees jobs only in the same Chrome as the Job Log, and its list only offers jobs dated 21 days before to 2 days after the photos. Dictation reads the Job Log on the same phone only. Rooms from the last visit only know visits dictated on that phone.
  • Photo links still use wellingtonparker.com (they work). The new photos.wellington-ps.co.uk address isn't set up.
  • Extract job details only reads a clean address line. When the address is buried in a sentence, type it in.
  • Two devices could give the same job number to two new jobs if both add one before syncing. Sync before adding jobs on a second device.
  • The iPhone can clear Dictation's saved notes if it isn't opened for about 7 days. Copy notes to Claude the same day.
