How it works: photos are split into jobs by the gaps between them, and each set is matched to the Job Log by where the photos were taken. Check each job, then click the first photo of each room and pick the area — it carries on until the next room you mark. Keys in the photo panel: ← → move, R rotate, Del remove photo, Esc close. Shift-click selects a run of photos to rotate together.
