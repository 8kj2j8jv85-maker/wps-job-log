How this works: fill in the job, tap a room, tap the microphone and talk. Tap the mic again to stop. Tap the next room and carry on. At the end tap Copy for Claude and paste into the WPS project chat. Jobs (top) starts a new job or opens an old one. A property you've done before on this phone gets its rooms from the last visit (a check-out uses the check-in); More rooms shows the full list. Tap ? to hide these hints.
